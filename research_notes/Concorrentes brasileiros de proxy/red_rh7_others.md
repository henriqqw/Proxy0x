## Market Insights

### Price Ranges (BRL, 2026)
- **Residential rotating**: R$ 4,90 - R$ 28,80/GB (cheapest: RH7/GM; most expensive: Proxy Roque)
- **IPv4 dedicated**: R$ 14,49 - R$ 33,00/IP/month
- **IPv6 dedicated**: R$ 5,00 - R$ 24,90/IP/month
- **Mobile 4G/5G**: R$ 12,50/GB (RH7) to R$ 44,80/IP/month (Roque)
- **Datacenter**: R$ 0,20 - R$ 0,29/IP (bulk) to R$ 4-15/IP/month (dedicated)

### Common Patterns
1. **PIX is universal**: Every Brazilian proxy seller accepts PIX; most make it the primary or only payment method
2. **No monthly fees**: Per-GB pay-as-you-go is the dominant model for residential proxies
3. **GB never expires**: Multiple sellers emphasize this as a differentiator vs. international providers that expire credits
4. **Anti-detect browser compatibility**: All sellers advertise compatibility with AdsPower, Multilogin, Dolphin Anty, etc.
5. **Target audience convergence**: Nearly all target Facebook/Meta Ads contingency operators and traffic managers
6. **Portuguese-language support**: Emphasized as a differentiator vs. international providers
7. **Upstream provider sharing**: Multiple sellers claim "90M+ IPs" suggesting they resell from the same upstream network
8. **IOF as selling point**: Brazilian sellers highlight the IOF tax (6.38%) and card spread that apply when paying international providers in USD

### Competitive Positioning
- **Premium/established**: Proxy Roque (high price, YouTube brand, 7 years)
- **Corporate/enterprise**: ProxyBR (CNPJ, physical address, Reclame Aqui, scraping API)
- **Feature-complete/mid-price**: Allan Proxys (API, granular targeting, 4 years, SMS cross-sell)
- **Price leaders**: RH7 Proxys, GM Proxys (R$ 4.90/GB starting)
- **CPA niche**: CPA Proxys (bots, courses, community)
- **Mobile niche**: NafeProxys (unlimited mobile data, very small)
- **Unknown/unverifiable**: Red Proxys (no extractable data)

### Red Flags Across the Market
- Several sellers show no CNPJ (Red Proxys, GM Proxys, CPA Proxys, Luma Proxys, Proxy Brasil)
- Price inconsistencies on the same website (RH7 dual domains, Luma Proxys)
- Claims of "90M+ IPs" by multiple small Brazilian sellers are almost certainly from shared upstream providers
- Self-reported review counts (Luma's "567 verified reviews" with no named platform)
- The UnovaCursos "comparison" article is sponsored content promoting ProxyAds, not an independent analysis
