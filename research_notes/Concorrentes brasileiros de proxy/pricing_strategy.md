## 2. Brazilian Customer Price Expectations by Use Case

### Takeaway
Brazilian SMB customers expect to pay R$5-15/GB for residential proxies paid via PIX. International card payments add 10-15% in fees (IOF + spread), making local BRL/PIX sellers strongly preferred. Use cases range from R$50-100/month for individual social media users to R$500-2000+/month for agencies and scrapers.

### Cited Findings
- RH7 Proxys article estimates the Brazilian residential proxy market at R$5-25/GB when paid in PIX, vs US$1.50-12/GB from international providers, which after IOF (6.38%) and card spread (~4%) translates to R$10-90/GB — [rh7proxys.com](https://rh7proxys.com/blog/quanto-custa-proxy-residencial-brasil-2026)
- A worked example: US$5/GB paid by international card at R$5.80/USD = R$32/GB after fees, versus R$10/GB paid directly in PIX — [rh7proxys.com](https://rh7proxys.com/blog/quanto-custa-proxy-residencial-brasil-2026)
- GTI Proxy's most popular plan appears to be the Movel Mensal at R$200/month (60 GB = R$3.33/GB effective) — [gtiproxy.com](https://gtiproxy.com/)
- CyberYozh warns that per-GB pricing can get expensive for high-traffic Instagram automation — [cyberyozh.com](https://app.cyberyozh.com/pt/blog/best-instagram-proxies-for-agencies-and-media-buyers/)

### Estimated Monthly Spend by Use Case

| Use Case | Estimated GB/month | Budget Range (BRL) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social media automation (1-5 accounts) | 2-5 GB | R$ 30-75 | Low bandwidth, high IP quality needed |
| Social media agency (20-50 accounts) | 10-30 GB | R$ 100-450 | Needs multiple clean IPs |

### Inferences
- Individual users (social media, small scraping) are very price-sensitive below R$100/month.
- Agencies tolerate R$200-500/month if the service is reliable and has good support.
- Heavy scrapers are the most volume-sensitive — they will compare per-GB prices aggressively.
- PIX eliminates a 10-15% fee disadvantage vs international competitors, making any local BRL price instantly more competitive.

### Gaps
- No Brazilian forum or Reddit posts with specific budget figures were found. The user budget estimates above are inferred from plan structures and general SaaS pricing patterns, not survey data.

## 5. COMPETITIVE POSITIONING ANALYSIS

### Can you undercut ALL Brazilian competitors while maintaining 40%+ margin?

**Answer: Partially.** You can undercut most competitors on per-GB pricing while maintaining 40%+ margin on the Basico tier. The Pro and Enterprise tiers require DataImpulse volume discounts to reach 40%.

| Competitor | Their Price | Your Price (effective) | Cheaper? | Your Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Allan Proxys | R$ 6,99/GB | Basico: R$ 9,98/GB | NO | 46.4% |
| Allan Proxys | R$ 6,99/GB | Pro: R$ 7,50/GB | NO | 29.5-43.8% |
| RH7 Proxys | R$ 5-15/GB | Basico: R$ 9,98/GB | Middle | 46.4% |
| Proxy Brasil | R$ 18-25/GB | ALL tiers | YES | 24-46% |
| Agencia Roque | R$ 28,80/GB | ALL tiers | YES | 24-46% |
| ShieldProx IPv4 | R$ 35/IP/mo | Different model | N/A | N/A |
| GTI Proxy Movel | R$ 3,33-15/GB | Enterprise: R$7/GB | NO (mobile) | 24-39% |
| FastProxy IPv4 | R$ 39,90/IP/mo | Different model | N/A | N/A |
| Bright Data | ~R$ 20/GB | ALL tiers | YES | 24-46% |

**Key insight:** Allan Proxys (R$6,99/GB) and RH7 (from R$5/GB) are the price leaders. You CANNOT undercut them at $1/GB upstream cost while maintaining healthy margins. However, you can position at R$7-10/GB and still undercut 70%+ of the market.

### Optimal Pricing Sweet Spot

**R$ 7,50-9,90/GB** is the sweet spot because:
1. It undercuts most Brazilian competitors (Proxy Brasil, Agencia Roque, ShieldProx, FastProxy, Bright Data)
2. It is competitive with Allan Proxys and RH7 (within 10-40%)
3. It maintains 30-46% gross margins
4. It is decisively cheaper than international providers paying in USD

### Pricing Model: Per GB, Per Month, or Hybrid?

**Recommendation: Hybrid (monthly subscription with GB included + overage)**

Reasons:
1. **Predictable revenue** — Monthly subscriptions create MRR (monthly recurring revenue)
2. **Lower perceived cost** — "R$49,90/mes" sounds cheaper than "R$9,98/GB x 5GB"
3. **Customer retention** — Subscriptions create stickiness
4. **Competitive differentiation** — Most Brazilian per-GB sellers (Allan, RH7, Proxy Brasil) have no subscription model; you'd be offering a more "professional" service
5. **Overage captures heavy users** — Users who exceed their plan pay per-GB overage, which has healthy margins

### Free Tier / Trial Strategy

**Recommended: 500 MB free trial (no credit card required)**

| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500 MB free trial | Low risk, lets users test | May attract tire-kickers | ~R$2,55/trial |
| 1 GB free trial | Better testing experience | Higher abuse risk | ~R$5,10/trial |
| 24h unlimited trial | Demonstrates confidence | High abuse potential | Unpredictable |
| 7-day money-back guarantee | No waste, builds trust | Refund processing overhead | Zero upfront |

**Best approach:** Offer 500 MB free (like GTI Proxy's 15-min test, but more generous) + 7-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. The combined cost of a free trial is R$2,55 per lead — if 1 in 5 converts, customer acquisition cost is R$12,75, which is excellent.

## 7. SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS

### If USD/BRL moves to R$5.50 (upstream becomes R$5.50/GB)

| Tier | Revenue | New Cost | New Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basico | R$ 49,90 | R$ 28,75 (5x5.50 + fees) | 42.4% |

1. Raise prices (risk: lose price-sensitive customers)

3. Absorb temporarily while building volume for DataImpulse discounts
