# Major Proxy Providers with Reseller/Whitelabel Programs (2025-2026)

## What are the reseller/whitelabel programs offered by each provider?

### Takeaway
Most major proxy providers offer some form of reseller or partner program, but the depth varies significantly. Dedicated reseller programs with full API and whitelabel support are offered by DataImpulse, Geonode, IPRoyal, NetNut, PacketStream, Proxying.io, and Rayobyte. Others like Bright Data, Oxylabs, and Decodo (formerly Smartproxy) focus more on affiliate/referral programs rather than true wholesale reseller arrangements.

### Cited Findings

**Bright Data**
- Offers a Partner Program (not a traditional reseller program) via PartnerStack with affiliate/referral tiers. Aimed at firms that resell, refer, or build on Bright Data's products. No fee to join. — [Bright Data Partners FAQ](https://brightdata.com/partners/faq); [PartnerStack Listing](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/brightdata)
- Partner program has multiple tiers including referral and solution partner types. FAQ and partner guide disagree on how many partner types exist (three vs. four). — [Bright Data Partner Program Guide PDF](https://brightdata.com/static/web/Bright-Data-Partner-Program-Guide.pdf)
- Affiliate program pays 50% revenue share, but sources conflict on the cap: PartnerStack listing says $1K per customer, while Bright Data's own affiliate page says $2.5K per customer. — [Bright Data Affiliate Page](https://brightdata.com/affiliate); [Affonso.io](https://affonso.io/affiliate-programs/bright-data)
- Pool of 400M+ IPs. Products include SERP API and Web Unlocker. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)

**Oxylabs**
- No public reseller or whitelabel program found. Search results returned no evidence of a reseller tier, white-label option, or partner signup page. — Based on extensive search across oxylabs.io and third-party review sites
- Oxylabs has 102M+ residential IPs from 195 countries, but appears to focus on direct enterprise sales rather than reseller channels. — [Comparitech](https://comparitech.com/proxies/oxylabs-proxy-service)

**IPRoyal**
- Reseller tools unlock after a deposit of $200 or more. Includes a panel for managing sub-users plus API documentation supporting multiple programming languages. — [ProxyOmega Review](https://proxyomega.com/reviews/iproyal)
- Described as "reseller-friendly" but no public reseller discount tiers or margin details found. — Based on multiple third-party review sites
- Pool of 32M+ residential IPs across 195+ countries. Traffic never expires. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- Standard retail residential pricing: $7/GB (1 GB) down to $4.90/GB (50 GB). Enterprise from $1.75/GB at 10,000+ GB. — [G2 Pricing](https://g2.com/products/iproyal/pricing); [Proxidize Blog](https://proxidize.com/blog/iproyal-pricing/)

**Smartproxy / Decodo**
- Smartproxy rebranded as Decodo in 2025. Old reseller/partnership URLs now 404. — Confirmed via WebFetch redirect tests
- Has a Partners Guide page at decodo.com/partners-guide. Contact for partnerships: partnerships@decodo.com. — [Decodo Partners Guide](https://decodo.com/partners-guide)
- Affiliate program earns up to 50% commission, capped at $2,500 per new customer, runs on Impact platform. 60-day cookie. Sources conflict on exact terms. — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/smartproxy-affiliate-program); [Referly](https://www.referly.so/affiliate-programs/smartproxy)
- Separate "Refer a Friend" program: both parties get 30% of friend's first transaction. — [Decodo Referral FAQ](https://decodo.com/faq/billing/referral-program)
- Reseller volume approximately $2.5/GB, lower at larger commitments. Pool of 115M+ residential IPs. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)

**NetNut**
- Dedicated reseller program with a Reseller Portal for managing sub-users, account settings, and proxy functions. — [NetNut Resellers Page](https://netnut.io/resellers/); [NetNut Reseller Portal Documentation](https://help.netnut.io/netnut-documentation/reseller-portal)
- Reseller API documentation is available. Resellers can create sub-users and manage their usage. — [NetNut Help Docs](https://help.netnut.io/netnut-documentation/reseller-portal)
- No public pricing, commission terms, or minimum investment found.

**Webshare**
- No evidence of a reseller or whitelabel program found. Webshare focuses on standard proxy API access for developers and small businesses. — Based on extensive search; [PrivacySavvy Review](https://privacysavvy.com/reviews/proxy-service/webshare)
- Standard API available with token-based authentication. Retail pricing approximately $2.25-$3.50/GB. — [Comparitech](https://comparitech.com/proxies/webshare-proxy-service)

**PacketStream**
- Reseller API offered for whitelabel resale. Pricing starts at $1.00/GB wholesale. Resellers can set their own prices and customize the service. — [PacketStream Residential Proxies](https://packetstream.io/residential-proxies); [HostAdvice](https://hostadvice.com/proxy-servers/packetstream-review/)
- Resellers draw from the same proxy pool as PacketStream's own customers. — [HostAdvice Review](https://hostadvice.com/proxy-servers/packetstream-review/)
- Reviews are mixed. Proxyway calls the service "cheap residential proxies on life support" with a small pool and technical issues. — [Proxyway Review](https://proxyway.com/reviews/packetstream-proxies)
- Multiple services are already reselling PacketStream under their own brand, confirming whitelabel resale is active. — [Spur.us](https://spur.us/tags/PACKETSTREAM_PROXY)

**SOAX**
- No dedicated reseller program found. SOAX focuses on direct-to-customer sales. — Based on extensive search across soax.com and third-party sites
- Retail pricing: Starter tier at $3.60/GB (25 GB included, $90/month). Pay-as-you-go option available. — [SOAX Residential](https://soax.com/proxies/residential)
- 191M+ ethically sourced IPs. Recently rebooted product line with a headful browser service replacing their web scraping API. — [Proxyway](https://proxyway.com/?p=45445)

**Infatica**
- Has an established reseller program with API access, but pricing is volume-based and requires sales contact. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- Retail residential pricing: $6.00-$12.00/GB. Mobile from $8/GB on trial, down to $1/GB at volume. — [ProxyPrice](https://proxyprice.pages.dev/provider/infatica); [HostAdvice](https://hostadvice.com/proxy-servers/infatica-review/)
- Separate affiliate program: 10-30% recurring commissions, 90-day cookies, no minimum payout. — [PostAffiliatePro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/infatica-affiliate-program/); [RevShare.so](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/infatica)

### Inferences
- The proxy reseller market is split between providers that offer true wholesale/whitelabel programs (DataImpulse, Geonode, PacketStream, IPRoyal, NetNut) and those that primarily offer affiliate/referral programs (Bright Data, Decodo, SOAX).
- Oxylabs and Webshare do not appear to have any public reseller programs, likely focusing on direct enterprise sales and self-service respectively.

### Gaps
- Oxylabs may have enterprise partner arrangements not publicly listed; could not confirm without contacting sales.
- Webshare may have unlisted reseller options; their pricing page and documentation do not mention it.
- SOAX's product reboot may have changed their partner strategy; current status is unclear.


## What are the minimum investments required to become a reseller?

### Takeaway
Minimum investments range from as low as $5 (Proxy-Cheap) to $500/month recurring (Geonode), with most providers falling in the $50-$250 range for initial deposit. Several providers require sales contact for custom pricing.

### Cited Findings
- **DataImpulse:** $50 minimum deposit, balance does not expire. KYC and reseller agreement required. — [DataImpulse Reseller Program](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **Proxy-Cheap:** $5 minimum deposit, balance does not expire. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **IPRoyal:** $200 deposit to unlock reseller tools. — [ProxyOmega Review](https://proxyomega.com/reviews/iproyal)
- **Geonode:** $500/month minimum recurring revenue after a 3-month ramp-in period. — [Geonode Reseller Page](https://geonode.com/reseller)
- **Proxying.io:** $250 starting deposit. — [Proxying.io Whitelabel Page](https://www.proxying.io/white-label-proxy-reseller-program)
- **Bright Data:** No minimum to start earning (affiliate/partner program, not wholesale). — [Bright Data Partners FAQ](https://brightdata.com/partners/faq)
- **PacketStream:** No minimum deposit publicly listed; wholesale starts at $1.00/GB. — [PacketStream](https://packetstream.io/residential-proxies)
- **NetNut, Infatica, Decodo, SOAX:** Minimum investments not publicly listed; require sales contact. — Based on search across multiple sources

### Inferences
- For someone starting with a small budget (under $100), DataImpulse ($50) and Proxy-Cheap ($5) are the most accessible entry points.
- IPRoyal at $200 is a mid-range entry point with proven reseller tools.
- Geonode's $500/month minimum makes it suitable only for resellers who already have customers or guaranteed demand.

### Gaps
- PacketStream's minimum deposit/commitment for reseller access could not be confirmed.
- VoidMob's minimum is not stated publicly; pools are prepaid GB pools set up after sales conversation.


## What pricing models do they use?

### Takeaway
The dominant pricing model is per-GB for rotating residential/mobile proxies, with per-IP pricing for static/datacenter proxies. Wholesale rates range from $0.50/GB (Geonode at 10TB+) to $2.50/GB (Decodo), with most providers offering volume-based tiered pricing.

### Cited Findings
- **Per GB (rotating residential/mobile):**
  - DataImpulse: ~$1/GB residential, ~$0.50/GB datacenter, ~$2/GB mobile wholesale — [DataImpulse](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
  - PacketStream: from $1.00/GB wholesale — [PacketStream](https://packetstream.io/residential-proxies)
  - IPRoyal: from ~$1.75/GB pay-as-you-go, lower at volume — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
  - Decodo: ~$2.50/GB reseller volume — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
  - VoidMob: ~$2/GB wholesale for mobile proxies — [VoidMob Blog](https://voidmob.com/blog/how-to-resell-proxies-2026)
  - Geonode: $0.83-$0.67/GB under 100 GB; $0.50/GB at 10TB+ — [Geonode Reseller](https://geonode.com/reseller)
  - Proxy-Seller: $0.70-$3.50/GB depending on volume — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)

- **Per IP (static/datacenter):**
  - Proxying.io ISP: from $2.25/IP; dedicated datacenter from $1.50/IP — [Proxying.io](https://www.proxying.io/white-label-proxy-reseller-program)
  - IPRoyal datacenter: from $1.39/proxy for 90 days; ISP from $2.40/proxy for 90 days — [G2](https://g2.com/products/iproyal/pricing)
  - Rayobyte datacenter: from $1/IP with custom tiers for larger orders — [Proxyway](https://proxyway.com/reviews/rayobyte-proxies)

- **Revenue share (affiliate/referral):**
  - Bright Data: 50% revenue share per active customer — [Bright Data Affiliate](https://brightdata.com/affiliate)
  - Decodo affiliate: up to 50% commission, capped at $2,500/customer — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/smartproxy-affiliate-program)
  - Infatica affiliate: 10-30% recurring — [PostAffiliatePro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/infatica-affiliate-program/)

### Inferences
- The per-GB model dominates for residential and mobile proxies, making it easy to calculate margins.
- At wholesale, the price floor for residential proxies appears to be around $0.50-$1.00/GB at very high volume, with $1.50-$2.50/GB being more typical for mid-volume resellers.
- Datacenter/static proxies use per-IP pricing, typically $1-$3/IP depending on dedication level and duration.

### Gaps
- NetNut's reseller pricing model could not be determined from public sources.
- Infatica's reseller rates are negotiated per partner; no public rate card exists.


## What whitelabel features do they offer?

### Takeaway
True whitelabel features (custom dashboard, custom branding, branded API) are offered by a subset of providers. Geonode, DataImpulse, Proxying.io, PacketStream, and HydraProxy explicitly offer whitelabel capabilities. Most others offer API access that enables building your own frontend, but not a ready-made branded dashboard.

### Cited Findings
- **Geonode:** Whitelabel dashboard included. Reseller API supports sub-account management, bandwidth allocation, usage metering, and customer provisioning. Resellers sell under their own brand. — [Geonode Reseller](https://geonode.com/reseller)
- **DataImpulse:** Whitelabel on your own domain. Dedicated reseller API and dashboard with real-time allocation and analytics. — [DataImpulse](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **Proxying.io:** Sell under your own brand. Full reseller API for automating sales. Dashboard for viewing, monitoring, and topping up accounts. — [Proxying.io](https://www.proxying.io/white-label-proxy-reseller-program)
- **PacketStream:** Whitelabel resale via Reseller API. Resellers can rebrand and set their own prices. — [PacketStream](https://packetstream.io/residential-proxies)
- **HydraProxy:** Resellers control branding. "Integration capabilities" mentioned but not detailed. — [ProxyCoupons](https://proxy.coupons/proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **VoidMob:** API-based; reseller handles storefront and branding. VoidMob does not contact end customers or provide a branded panel. — [VoidMob Blog](https://voidmob.com/blog/how-to-resell-proxies-2026)
- **NetNut:** Reseller Portal for managing sub-users. API documentation available. Whitelabel branding capabilities not explicitly confirmed. — [NetNut Docs](https://help.netnut.io/netnut-documentation/reseller-portal)
- **IPRoyal:** Panel for managing sub-users and API documentation. Whitelabel branding not explicitly mentioned. — [ProxyOmega](https://proxyomega.com/reviews/iproyal)
- **Rayobyte:** "White-label options" mentioned in one review but not detailed. API access confirmed. — [EarthWeb](https://earthweb.com/proxy-reseller-programs/)

### Inferences
- Providers with the most complete whitelabel offerings (branded dashboard + API + custom domain) are DataImpulse and Geonode.
- Several providers offer API-only reseller access (IPRoyal, NetNut, VoidMob), meaning you must build your own customer-facing dashboard.
- The distinction between "reseller API" and "whitelabel" is important: a reseller API lets you manage sub-users programmatically, while whitelabel adds branded UI and custom domain support.

### Gaps
- Detailed whitelabel customization options (logo placement, color themes, domain mapping) were not found for most providers.
- Whether NetNut's reseller portal can be fully whitelabeled could not be confirmed from public documentation.


## Which providers offer the best reseller margins?

### Takeaway
Geonode explicitly advertises 30-40%+ margins. PacketStream's low $1/GB wholesale enables the highest potential markup. DataImpulse at ~$1/GB also offers strong margin potential. The actual margin depends on what you charge your end customers and your volume tier.

### Cited Findings
- **Geonode:** Explicitly advertises 30-40%+ margins at wholesale rates. Includes a margin calculator on their reseller page. — [Geonode Reseller](https://geonode.com/reseller)
- **PacketStream:** Wholesale at $1.00/GB. If retail market rate is $3-5/GB, potential margin is 66-80%. — [PacketStream](https://packetstream.io/residential-proxies)
- **DataImpulse:** Wholesale at ~$1/GB residential. Similar margin potential to PacketStream at retail rates. — [DataImpulse](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **Proxy-Cheap:** Volume discounts: 5% at $500, 10% at $1,000, 15% at $5,000, 20% at $15,000. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **VoidMob:** Example scenario: $2/GB wholesale, $4/GB retail = $2/GB margin (50%). At 500 GB/month = $1,000 gross margin. — [VoidMob Blog](https://voidmob.com/blog/how-to-resell-proxies-2026)
- **Bright Data affiliate:** 50% revenue share per customer, capped at $1K-$2.5K per customer (sources disagree on cap). — [Bright Data Affiliate](https://brightdata.com/affiliate); [PartnerStack](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/brightdata)

### Inferences
- The lowest wholesale prices (PacketStream at $1/GB, DataImpulse at $1/GB) offer the highest potential margins if you can sell at market rates ($3-5/GB).
- However, PacketStream's quality issues (small pool, technical problems) may make it harder to retain customers, eroding effective margins.
- Geonode's explicitly stated 30-40% margin is achievable but requires the $500/month minimum commitment.
- Affiliate/referral programs (Bright Data, Decodo) offer high percentage commissions but are capped and do not give you control over pricing or customer relationships.

### Gaps
- IPRoyal and NetNut's actual reseller wholesale rates are not public, making margin calculation impossible without contacting sales.
- Real-world margin data from actual resellers was not found in any source.

## What are the contract terms and lock-in periods?

### Takeaway
Most reseller programs do not publicly disclose contract lengths or lock-in periods. The trend is toward pay-as-you-go or deposit-based models without long-term commitments, but some providers (Geonode) require monthly minimums.

### Cited Findings
- **DataImpulse:** Balance does not expire. KYC and reseller agreement required. No public contract length or termination terms. — [DataImpulse](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **Proxy-Cheap:** Balance does not expire. KYC form and reseller agreement required. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **Geonode:** $500/month minimum recurring revenue after 3-month ramp-in period. Must follow Acceptable Use Policy and reseller agreement. Reselling to other resellers requires written approval. Contract length and termination terms not specified. — [Geonode Reseller](https://geonode.com/reseller)
- **IPRoyal:** Traffic never expires (non-expiring balance). — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **Proxying.io:** Reseller access manually enabled after approval. No contract length or termination terms disclosed. — [Proxying.io](https://www.proxying.io/white-label-proxy-reseller-program)
- **VoidMob:** No KYC required. Crypto payments. Unused GB return to pool when customer package expires. No contract length or termination mentioned. — [VoidMob Blog](https://voidmob.com/blog/how-to-resell-proxies-2026)
- **Bright Data:** No fee to join partner program. — [Bright Data Partners FAQ](https://brightdata.com/partners/faq)

### Inferences
- The proxy reseller market generally favors flexible, low-commitment models -- most programs are deposit/balance based rather than subscription-locked.
- Non-expiring balances (DataImpulse, IPRoyal, Proxy-Cheap) reduce risk for new resellers who may take time to build a customer base.
- Geonode is the only provider with a clear ongoing minimum commitment ($500/month), making it the highest-risk option for small starters.
- KYC requirements vary: some providers (DataImpulse, Proxy-Cheap) require it, while others (VoidMob) explicitly do not.

### Gaps
- Contract terms for Bright Data's partner program (beyond "no fee to join") are not public.
- Oxylabs, NetNut, Infatica, and Decodo's contract terms for resellers could not be determined from public sources.
- Termination policies and refund terms are not publicly documented for any provider examined.


## Summary: Providers Most Suitable for Small Budget

### Takeaway
For someone starting with a small budget, the most accessible options are Proxy-Cheap ($5 minimum), DataImpulse ($50 minimum), and IPRoyal ($200 minimum). PacketStream offers the lowest wholesale rate ($1/GB) but has quality concerns.

### Cited Findings
- **Proxy-Cheap:** $5 minimum deposit, non-expiring balance, positioned for startups and small businesses. Entry rate ~$1.5/GB. — [DataImpulse Comparison](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **DataImpulse:** $50 minimum deposit, non-expiring balance. Full whitelabel on your own domain. ~$1/GB residential wholesale. — [DataImpulse](https://dataimpulse.com/blog/best-proxy-reseller-programs/)
- **PacketStream:** $1/GB wholesale, whitelabel API. However, Proxyway warns of small pool and technical issues. — [PacketStream](https://packetstream.io/residential-proxies); [Proxyway](https://proxyway.com/reviews/packetstream-proxies)
- **IPRoyal:** $200 to unlock reseller tools. Reseller panel and API. Non-expiring traffic. — [ProxyOmega](https://proxyomega.com/reviews/iproyal)
- **Proxying.io:** $250 starting deposit. Full whitelabel. — [Proxying.io](https://www.proxying.io/white-label-proxy-reseller-program)

### Inferences
- A practical starting path would be: begin with DataImpulse or Proxy-Cheap to test the market with minimal risk, then scale to IPRoyal or Geonode as customer base grows.
- PacketStream's low cost is attractive but quality issues may lead to customer churn, making it risky as a primary supplier.
- Building your own dashboard/frontend (using provider APIs) costs development time but gives you full brand control and customer ownership.
- The affiliate/referral route (Bright Data, Decodo) requires zero investment but gives you no control over pricing, customer relationships, or product differentiation.

### Gaps
- Actual customer satisfaction data for resellers of each provider was not found.
- Independent quality comparisons of proxy pools at wholesale/reseller tier were not available.
